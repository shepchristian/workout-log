# Fitness Plan

**August 2026 – September 2027**

> **Goal:** Be as healthy as possible by September 2027, with a visible six-pack as one benchmark.

## 1. Overview and Constraints

This plan covers nutrition, running and cycling benchmarks, and a full-body HIIT/CrossFit-style lifting split, phased across five blocks from August 2026 to September 2027.

### Training Constraints

- 3–5 workouts per week, flexible weekly rhythm
- Lifting capped at approximately 50 minutes per session
- Running: outdoor preferred, minimal indoor
- Cycling: indoor or outdoor
- Equipment: full gym plus home access (barbell, dumbbell, kettlebell, rower); no rings; TRX available

### Starting Point (June 2026)

- **5K:** 21:19
- **10K:** 45:50 predicted
- **Half marathon:** 1:44:48 predicted
- **VO2 max:** 55 (Excellent, top 10%)
- **Cycling VO2 max:** 56 (Superior, top 5%)
- **Estimated FTP:** approximately 250–260 W
  - 20–30 minute power: 267–282 W
  - 1-hour power: 242 W, dated from 2021
- **5-minute power:** 344 W
- **Body composition goal:** Lose 15–25 lb for visible abs

## 2. Nutrition Approach

No calorie counting long-term. Track loosely for 1–2 weeks at the start to understand current protein intake and habits, then shift to a food-quality, plate-method approach.

### Foods to Emphasize

- Protein at every meal: chicken, fish, eggs, Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, lean beef, tofu, or tempeh
- Vegetables, especially volume-heavy options: leafy greens, broccoli, peppers, and cucumber
- Whole, minimally processed carbohydrates: rice, oats, potatoes, and fruit, timed around training
- Healthy fats in moderation: avocado, olive oil, and nuts
- Fiber-rich foods: beans, lentils, and whole grains

### Simple Plate Method

- Approximately 1 palm-to-fist portion of protein
- Approximately half the plate as vegetables
- Approximately 1 cupped hand of carbohydrates, with more on training days
- Approximately 1 thumb of fats

### Deprioritize, Not Off-Limits

- Liquid calories: alcohol, sugary drinks, and juice
- Fried foods
- Mindless snacking on packaged or processed foods

**Exception:** Desserts are unrestricted and indulgent, not health-optimized.

## 6. Lifting and HIIT Split

Full-body each session, twice per week, approximately 50 minutes, balancing strength and conditioning equally.

### Session Structure

1. **Warm-up:** 5 minutes of dynamic mobility and ramp-up sets
2. **Strength block:** 20–25 minutes of compound lifts
3. **Core block:** 8–10 minutes focused on posture, running, and cycling
4. **Conditioning finisher:** 10–15 minutes, metcon style
5. **Cooldown:** 5 minutes

### Day A: Squat and Press Bias

#### Strength

- Back squat: 4 sets × 5 reps
- Push press: 3 sets × 6 reps

#### Core: 3 Rounds

- Hollow hold: 45 seconds
- Pallof press: 10 reps per side
- Bird dog: 30 seconds per side

#### Finisher: 10–12 Minute AMRAP

- 10 kettlebell swings
- 10 box jumps or step-ups
- 200 m row
- 10 push-ups

### Day B: Hinge and Pull Bias

#### Strength

- Deadlift: 4 sets × 5 reps
- Pull-ups or TRX rows: 3 sets × 8 reps

#### Core: 3 Rounds

- Ab wheel rollouts or hanging leg raises: 10 reps
- Dead bugs: 10 reps per side
- Single-arm farmer carry: 30 m per side

#### Finisher: 10–12 Minute EMOM

- Minute 1: 12 wall balls or dumbbell thrusters
- Minute 2: 15 kettlebell swings
- Minute 3: 30-second rower sprint
- Repeat

Rotate Day A and Day B across the two lifting slots per week. Optionally add a short third, core-focused session when a fifth weekly workout slot is used.

### Core Work Rationale

- **Anti-extension:** Hollow holds, ab wheel rollouts, and hanging leg raises brace the torso for deadlifts and squats and stabilize the bike position.
- **Anti-rotation:** Pallof presses and farmer carries support running-stride efficiency and reduce energy-wasting torso twist.
- **Posture-specific:** Bird dogs and dead bugs stabilize the pelvis for running form and saddle position.

Visible definition still depends primarily on body-fat percentage, so nutrition is doing the heavy lifting. This core work supports function, including running economy, ride endurance, and injury prevention, as well as aesthetics.

### Lifting Progression by Phase

| Phase | Strength Focus | Conditioning Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Foundation | Re-establish working weights, perfect form | Moderate intensity, build work capacity |
| Hold | Maintain loads, no PR chasing | Stay consistent, lower priority than nutrition |
| Second Push | Progressive overload monthly | Shorten rest periods, increase intensity |
| Recomp | Push for strength PRs | Conditioning peaks here |
| Polish | Maintain strength, avoid overreach | Sharpen: fastest times and heaviest AMRAP scores |

## 7. Weekly Template: 3–5 Flexible Sessions

- **2 lifting sessions:** HIIT/CrossFit style, 50 minutes, rotating Day A and Day B
- **1–2 bike sessions:** One easy or steady aerobic ride and one harder interval ride
- **1 run:** Outdoor preferred, using intervals or steady state
- **Optional fifth session:** Extra conditioning, sport, or active recovery

> Revisit benchmarks at each phase transition and adjust based on actual progress.
